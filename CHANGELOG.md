# Changelog

What changed in each version of SevenSleep, newest first. A version is a heading, and small
fixes to it are listed under it as patches. Everything before 3.23 is in the
[git history](https://github.com/jalen-cheng/sevensleep/commits/main).

## 3.23

- Wi-Fi and hardware watchdogs for the Pod, off until switched on with `scripts/setup_watchdog.sh`.
- The SevenSleep lettering is the logo in the app and on the docs site; the icon is the favicon and Home Screen icon.
- The British Columbia time zone entry added in 3.22 is removed.
- A shorter README and this shorter changelog.
- Minor UI changes.
